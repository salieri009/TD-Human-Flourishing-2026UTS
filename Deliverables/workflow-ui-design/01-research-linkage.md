# Research Linkage (Design Justification)

Every step in this workflow maps to a specific empirical finding. Citations appear as `[Author Year]` next to the design element they justify.

- **Singh, Guan & Rieh (2025)** — five metacognitive cue types (Orienting, Monitoring, Comprehension, Broadening Perspectives, Consolidation); behavior-triggered delivery.
- **Jiang & Jiang (2024) Physics-STAR** — three-step tutoring loop: *Knowledge Explanation → Error Analysis → Review Suggestion*, repeated until mastery.
- **Exintaris, Karunaratne & Yuriev (2023) SMARTCHEMPer** — students critique AI-generated solutions instead of consuming them.
- **Marin Diaz (2025)** — three cognitive-behavioral clusters (Cluster 0 Moderate, Cluster 1 High-Confidence/Low-Reflection, Cluster 2 High-Reflection/Verification) with cluster-specific scaffolding.
- **Fan et al. (2025)** — 47% direct delegation; *Creating* (39.8%) and *Analysing* (30.2%) are the most-offloaded Bloom levels — friction scales with Bloom level.
