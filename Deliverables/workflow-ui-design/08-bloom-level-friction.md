## Bloom-Level Friction Calibration (Fan 2025)

Friction is **highest** where the offloading harm is highest:

| Bloom level | % of student delegation (Fan 2025) | Copilot behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Creating** | 39.8% | Full 5-cue + Physics-STAR loop, Critique path encouraged |
| **Analysing** | 30.2% | Full 5-cue + Physics-STAR loop |
| **Applying / Evaluating** | ~12.9% combined | Orienting + Monitoring + Consolidation |
| **Remembering / Understanding** | small | Light Orienting only; near-instant answer permitted |

This makes the inverted-Bloom problem the design fulcrum.
