## Cue Dictionary (lift directly into slide as a table)

| Cue (Singh 2025) | Trigger | Wording on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Orienting** | Session start (1×) | *"Think about the qualities you want Copilot's response to have."* |
| 🟡 **Monitoring** | Immediately after AI response (≤ 3×) | *"How closely does the response align with what you expected? Did you find anything unexpected?"* |
| 🟡 **Comprehension** | Inactivity / no follow-up (≤ 2×) | *"Is there anything you do not fully understand? If yes, explore the sources or ask follow-up questions."* |
| 🟡 **Broadening Perspectives** | Single-viewpoint focus detected (≤ 2×) | *"What perspectives might you be missing from your current understanding?"* |
| 🟢 **Consolidation** | Task complete or 4 min before time-out | *"Write a summary of the main points you have learned about this topic."* |

Minimum 2–3 minutes between any two consecutive cues to avoid cognitive overload (Singh 2025, p. 4).
